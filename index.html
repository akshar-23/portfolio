About Me


I am a dedicated Game Developer and Designer with a strong passion for creating impactful, immersive game experiences. Currently pursuing a MS in Game Science and Design, I focus on blending creativity with technical skills to design games that engage and challenge players on multiple levels. My experience spans core gameplay, game mechanics, physics, cloud management, and user interactions, and I've developed diverse projects in both Unity and Unreal, specializing in elements that bring depth and innovation to each game.

Beyond game development, I am deeply interested in the possibilities at the intersection of artificial intelligence and games. With experience in machine learning and AI tools in Python, I envision a future where these technologies play a significant role in creating games that are not only more intelligent but capable of meaningful, positive impact. My goal is to explore and push the boundaries of AI within game development, aiming to craft experiences that go beyond entertainment to offer something truly unique and beneficial for players and the industry.

I am also committed to collaboration and have had the opportunity to lead technical teams and organize major events, helping me develop strong project management and team-building skills. Looking ahead, I am excited to contribute to the evolution of game design, combining AI and innovative gameplay to create experiences that can redefine what games can accomplish.

PROJECTS

Cricket Tycoon*
Developed Cricket Tycoon, a social card collection and sports management game, implementing core gameplay mechanics, state machines, and interactive player systems. Integrated Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Functions for dynamic inventory management and pseudo-multiplayer features. Conducted alpha testing with 100+ users, achieving 85% satisfaction and 40% reduction in bug reports. Built a scalable system handling 1000+ concurrent users with sub-100ms response times, enhancing player engagement and stability.
Smil-E-Mart
Created Smil-E-Mart, a narrative-driven simulation game where players uncover societal mysteries as a robotic cashier in a post-apocalyptic world. Designed core mechanics like item scanning, emoji-based dialogue, and branching storylines with 10+ decision points. Implemented systems to flag suspicious behaviors, influencing narrative outcomes. Achieved 90% player comprehension in playtests, with an average session length of 30 minutes, blending themes of morality, agency, and human connection.
O2xygen*
Developing O2xygen, a 3D first-person roguelike cave exploration game in Unreal Engine. Features include procedural map generation, underwater physics, and AI-driven creatures across 5 biomes. Built a modular combat system supporting 8 weapon types and an inventory framework for 20+ items. Focused on immersive exploration challenges and dynamic environmental hurdles, creating a unique and engaging player experience.
Tic Tac Toe: Endless Fun Game
Designed and launched Tic Tac Toe: Endless Fun Game, a reimagined version of the classic game with unique mechanics and 3 AI difficulty modes. Enhanced replayability by moving beyond the traditional solved format, ensuring long-term user interest. Successfully published on the Google Play Store, showcasing innovative features and engaging gameplay for players of all ages.
Movie Recommendation System
Engineered a Python-based recommendation system using the MovieLens dataset (100K entries) to suggest personalized movies, achieving 85% accuracy in predictive modeling. Designed algorithms to enhance relevance and user engagement, delivering tailored movie suggestions for an improved user experience.
Face Recognition System
Developed a machine learning model for real-time face detection with 95% accuracy across varied lighting conditions using OpenCV. Integrated webcam functionality for smooth and reliable detection, making it suitable for diverse applications such as security and user authentication.
