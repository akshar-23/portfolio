- Extended the Sturgeon constraint-based level generator (Python, PySAT) to spacetime levels, running **100–500× faster** than Spacetime WFC.
- Built an interactive editor for changing levels and paths in real time, with 3D voxel views through VTK / ParaView.
- Added 4D spacetime blending of learned game mechanics along orthogonal planes.
- Benchmarked speed and solvability across three domains; published at **AAAI AIIDE 2025** and **FDG 2026**.
