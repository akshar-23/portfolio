An open-source suite for 2D, 3D-blended and physics-based level generation, with an interactive editor and VTK 3D views. It grew out of extending the Sturgeon level generator to generate levels through both space and time.

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Overview">

  Levels are modelled as constraint problems, so the generator can guarantee they are solvable. The editor lets you change a level or a path and see the result in real time.

  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Under the hood">

  - Constraint models in **PySAT**, and continuous spacetime gameplay solved with **Z3 SMT**
  - 4D spacetime blending of learned game mechanics
  - Generation speed and solvability benchmarked across the three domains with **Pandas**, surfacing per-domain performance trade-offs
  - 3D voxel views of generated levels through **VTK / ParaView**
