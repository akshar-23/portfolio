- You play the support of an RPG party: your spells change how the **AI allies and enemies** behave, instead of you dealing the damage.
- Three tools: a **healing circle** for your allies, a **stun** to lock enemies down, and a **smokescreen** to scatter a group.
- The fight is about positioning: keep your melee ally alive and in reach, so they can land the damage needed to win.
- A free Windows build is on itch.io.
